## Description[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span)
Discretionary macro volatility overlay engine integrating quantitative GARCH(1,1) / LSTM implied volatility predictions with central bank catalyst event windows (Fed, ECB, SARB)[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

---

## 📌 Architecture & Strategy[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)

This framework bridges quantitative volatility forecasting with human discretionary execution[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span):

1. **Quantitative Signal Layer:** Ingests statistical IV forecasts generated by machine learning/GARCH models and calculates basis-point mispricings relative to market-implied volatility[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
2. **Discretionary Macro Filter:** Evaluates policy divergence, regime shifts, and central bank event windows to determine option strategy selection (Straddles, Strangle, Calendar Spreads, or Tactical Pass)[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
3. **Risk & Execution Control:** Applies regime-dependent position sizing and conviction ratings to prevent trading into liquidity voids or binary structural breaks[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
